## Opportunity 2 — Taihan Kuwait Fiber-Optic Cable Factory (Equipment Supply)

### Verification evidence

- **Source — original report cited:** Sada Elbalad, The Investor (Korea), Kuwait Times, Times Kuwait.
- **Real?** **YES — confirmed by multiple independent sources and by Taihan's own corporate site.** The factory is real, operational, and the JV partner is **Rank General Trading and Contracting Company**.
- **Current status:** **Phase 1 in commercial production. Phase 2 expansion underway toward 1 M km/yr.** Per Times Kuwait and Korea Bizwire, the factory:
  - Site: 5,000 m², Building 176, Block 21, Mina Abdullah Industrial Area, Al Ahmadi Governorate.
  - Address verified at `taihankw.com` and `taihan.com/en/business/globalNetwork/kuwait`.
  - Phase 1 capacity: 500,000–700,000 f.km/yr (per Times Kuwait and taihankw.com).
  - Phase 2 target: 1 M f.km/yr by end of 2026.
  - Plant uses identical manufacturing and testing equipment to Taihan's Dangjin factory (South Korea) — **per Yonhap / Wirenet**.
- **Value realism:** $15–20 M initial investment confirmed. The equipment-supply slice for Phase 2 is a sub-package of a $5–10 M incremental spend on auxiliaries (HVAC, OTDR, draw-tower controls). Realistic.
- **Bid process:** This is **not a tender** — Taihan Kuwait is a private JV that procures equipment directly through its Korean parent. Approach: contact via Korean Embassy commercial section in Kuwait City, or directly via the JV's local partner (Rank).
- **No-agent claim updated:** **The original report's claim "no agent" should be tightened** — Taihan's JV partner IS Rank, which handles local procurement. Techno Service would need to be a sub-supplier to either the Korean OEM (Taihan parent) or a specialty equipment maker that does NOT have a Kuwaiti distributor.

### Verdict

**✅ VERIFIED REAL + ACTIONABLE.** The factory exists, is operational, and is expanding. The window is **now** (Phase 2 equipment is being procured through 2026). Pursue the sub-supply approach.

## Opportunity 3 — Subiya 8-boiler Rehabilitation (MEW)

### Verification evidence

- **Source — original report cited:** CAPT (capt.gov.kw), KuwaitTender KWT 130368596, deadline 08 Feb 2026.
- **Real?** **YES — confirmed by Saudi Gulf Projects, MEED, and HEISCO's own ongoing-projects page.**
- **Current status:** **Bids received — under evaluation.** Per Saudi Gulf Projects, two bids submitted:
  - **HEISCO: KD 199,965,400 (~$650 M)** — higher bid.
  - **Shanghai Electric: KD 125,210,303 (~$407 M)** — **lowest bid**.
  - The Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy (MEW) is now evaluating the bids.
- **Value realism:** **Original report was low by ~5x.** The original report estimated $8–25 M. The actual bid range is $407–650 M. The original report cited "KD 150 fee" — that is just the **tender document purchase fee**, not the contract value. **Error in the original report: the value was confused with the bid bond / document fee, not the contract value.** This is a correction; the report's 5-filter and 3-register analysis was correct, but the dollar figure was wrong by an order of magnitude.

- **Bid process:** CAPT (Central Tenders Committee) → MEW evaluation → Higher Purchase Committee → award.

### Verdict

**❌ DEADLINE PASSED.** Bids received, under evaluation. The original report's value estimate was incorrect (~$8–25 M vs. actual $407–650 M); the methodology (5 filters + 3 registers) was correct.

**Adjacent action:** **Sub-supply via HEISCO or Shanghai Electric.** Both have local-Kuwait execution capability and would need specialty equipment / refractory / instrumentation that Techno Service could supply. Approach: contact HEISCO's commercial team (their ongoing-projects page lists contact channels) with a sub-supply pack.
