1. Manufacturer's own "Where to buy" / "Representatives" / "Find a distributor" pages.

4. The Gulf industrial-trader market (Mahatta Trading public LinkedIn portfolio — 22 named principals — was used as a negative cross-check; **any candidate appearing in Mahatta's portfolio is auto-rejected**).
