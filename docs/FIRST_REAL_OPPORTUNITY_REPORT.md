## 1. Constitutional Framework

Each opportunity below is qualified by the **5 Filters** of the Phase 9
Proactive Discovery Charter (v2.4, PR-PD-001..003), and cleared by the
**3 Constitutional Registers** of Document 04 §Constitutional Architecture:

### 5 Filters (all 5 must PASS = QUALIFIED)

1. **Kuwait climate (55 °C, dust, salt air)** — the product must be
   rated for, or retrofittable to, the Gulf operating environment.
2. **Retrofit-friendly** — applies to existing Kuwaiti industrial
   assets; not green-field-only.
3. **No agent in Kuwait** — the manufacturer has no exclusive
   in-country representation that we would displace.
4. **Low operating cost** — operating economics compatible with
   Kuwaiti industrial buyers (per-tender, per-factory, not per-unit-sold).
5. **No specialised training** — commissioning and operation do not
   require a factory-certified engineer to be on-site permanently.

### 3 Registers (any 1 hit = REJECTED at register layer)

- **RESTRICTED** — industries Techno Service is forbidden to represent
  (defence, weapons, gambling, alcohol, etc.).
- **CONFLICT** — industries that conflict with existing represented
  principals on the `represented_principals_register`.
- **NON_REPRESENTED** — sectors already covered by an active principal
  representation agreement (would require renegotiation).

### Initialisation

This report treats the Constitution v2.4 discovery framework as
**operational** and applies it manually, with the **5 filters** ANDed
and the **3 Registers** checked. Each opportunity's register verdict is
recorded in the table at §6.

## 2. Opportunity 1 — Power Provision for KOC Artificial Lift Pumps (Phase II)

**Product / Service name:** Switchgear, prefabricated electrical
substations, and SCADA-ready power-distribution skids for KOC's South
& East Kuwait (S&EK) artificial-lift pump network.

**Why it fits Kuwait (5 filters):**
1. **Climate** — Outdoor switchgear and substations at KOC's Burgan,
   Magwa, and Ahmadi fields routinely face 55 °C ambient + dust storms
   + salt-laden air. The product line must be IP66+ with ≥100 µm HDG
   coating (per desert-rated switchgear industry practice).
2. **Retrofit-friendly** — KOC's existing wells are being electrified
   in phases; switchgear replaces diesel-driven artificial-lift units
   in-place. No green-field pipeline work required.
3. **No agent in Kuwait** — International switchgear OEMs (ABB, Siemens,
   Schneider, Eaton) have agents; the mid-tier European and Korean
   switchgear suppliers typically do not.
4. **Low operating cost** — A packaged electrical skid is a one-time
   capital sale; no recurring consumables.
5. **No specialised training** — KOC's electrical maintenance crews
   are trained on standard IEC switchgear; commissioning follows KOC's
   own SAT procedures.

**Why it doesn't conflict (3 Registers):**
- **RESTRICTED:** Oil & gas upstream electrical infrastructure is not
  on the restricted list. ✓
- **CONFLICT:** No existing represented principal in the switchgear /
  substation segment. ✓
- **NON_REPRESENTED:** Industrial electrical distribution is not on
  the represented principals register. ✓

**Estimated value:** $30 – $80 M per substation package; the
Phase-II A programme is 3 packages (Areas 6/10/12, 7/9/11, 8/13)
totalling **$100 – $250 M** for the full KOC Phase II A scope. KOC's
2026 procurement plan totals $19.78 B (per CAPT-published
`111733116`).

## 3. Opportunity 2 — Taihan Kuwait Fiber-Optic Cable Factory (Equipment Supply)

sheathing-line control systems, OTDR-based quality-test stations,
and desert-rated HVAC for the cleanroom.

**Why it fits Kuwait (5 filters):**

   50 °C+ ambient + salt-air + dust loading. Factory HVAC and
   process cooling must operate at ambient ≥50 °C without derating.
2. **Retrofit-friendly** — The factory is a 5,000 m² greenfield
   **but** the Phase 2 expansion (target 1 M km/year by 2026) is a
   brownfield retrofit of the existing line. Auxiliaries are added

3. **No agent in Kuwait** — Taihan's JV partner is the local
   construction & trading firm **Rank** (مقاولات). Taihan does not
   have an industrial-equipment agent distinct from its EPC partner.
4. **Low operating cost** — Equipment sale is one-time capital;
   consumables (fiber lubricants, calibration gases) are recurring
   but small.
5. **No specialised training** — Modern draw-tower control systems
   have HMI-driven commissioning; Taihan's own Korean engineers
   can train the local operators.

**Why it doesn't conflict (3 Registers):**

column-tray replacement, and corrosion-monitoring instrumentation
on the MAB Sea Island hydrocarbon-handling trains.

**Why it fits Kuwait (5 filters):**

   salt spray, 50 °C+ ambient, high humidity. Materials and

   must be IP66+ and salt-spray-rated (e.g., NACE MR0175).
2. **Retrofit-friendly** — "Critical Revamp Works" is, by
   definition, in-place upgrade. Existing columns, vessels, and
   piping are retained; internals and instrumentation are
   replaced.

   contractors (e.g., Técnicas Reunidas, Saipem, Petrofac,
   smaller Indian EPCs) typically bid project-by-project and
   do not maintain permanent in-country offices.
4. **Low operating cost** — A revamp is a one-time capital
   project; the in-country operating cost is for the duration
   of the campaign (typically 12–24 months).
5. **No specialised training** — KNPC's own process operators
   run the plant; the contractor provides specialist welding,
   NDT, and instrumentation commissioning crews.

**Why it doesn't conflict (3 Registers):**
