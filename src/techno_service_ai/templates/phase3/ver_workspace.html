Document 06 §6 — Verification Workspace. Workspace for a single verification record: material, evidence, claim classifications, independence status, verifier, second reviewer, outcome.

Material claim: {{ claim_id }}

The verification record carries the producer identity, the verifier identity, the claim classification, the source citations, the outcome, and the second reviewer (for material claims). Producer == verifier is REJECTED (see tests/test_phase3.py::test_ac_p3_004_producer_not_equal_to_verifier_rejected).

Layout

  • Header: material claim, classification, producer, verifier
  • Evidence: source citations
  • Independence: producer ≠ verifier; Second Reviewer (if material)
  • Outcome: VALIDATED / QUALIFIED / REJECTED / PENDING
  • Actions: complete, reject, escalate, record outcome
